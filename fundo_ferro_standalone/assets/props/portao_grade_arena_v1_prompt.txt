PROMPT NANO BANANA (PORTÃO LEVADIÇO DA ARENA):
Game asset, 2.5D front view, underground dark industrial steampunk art style for a clandestine goblin arena. A heavy portcullis / iron gate barrier that closes off the colosseum entrance portal.
- Sturdy gate made of thick rusted dark iron bars, heavy rivets, crisscross reinforcement plates, and sharp pointed spikes along the bottom edge.
- Set between two vertical iron channel rails / guide tracks mounted on ancient damp stone wall pillars.
- Heavy steel chains and pulleys attached to the top corners for raising and lowering the gate.
- Strictly orthogonal front-facing perspective, perfectly horizontal ground baseline and horizontal iron crossbeams, no diagonal tilt, no 45 degree rotation.
- Isolated on a completely flat, solid, pure magenta (#FF00FF) background, completely uniform, no glow, no cast shadow on background, sharp edges, centered, full object visible with margin.
