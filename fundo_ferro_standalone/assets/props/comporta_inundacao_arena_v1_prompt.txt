PROMPT NANO BANANA (COMPORTA DE INUNDAÇÃO DA ARENA):
Game asset, 2.5D front view, matching the steampunk underground industrial art style of the reference image. A heavy cast-iron wall floodgate / sluice gate nozzle embedded in dark mossy stone wall for an underground goblin colosseum pit.
- Heavy rectangular riveted iron frame set into ancient stone masonry, seen directly from the front (perpendicular to camera, no diagonal tilt).
- Contains a vertical iron sliding gate valve (sluice gate) with heavy iron bars, copper corrosion, and a top brass manual screw wheel / gear mechanism to raise and lower the floodgate.
- Water discharge outlet leading into the fighting pit.
- Strict orthogonal perspective: horizontal stone wall, perfectly horizontal baseline, no 45 degree isometric distortion, no angled pipes.
- Isolated on a completely flat, solid, pure magenta (#FF00FF) background, completely uniform, no glow, no floor shadow on background, sharp edges, centered.
